Biological Projection Rule: Connections are made between CA3 and CA1 pyramidal cells based on anatomical region mapping:

  • Dorsal CA3 → Dorsal CA1
  • Ventral CA3 → Ventral CA1
  • Proximal CA3 → Proximal CA1
  • Distal CA3 → Distal CA1

Synapse probability is derived from Hippocampome.org and scaled to mouse-specific anatomy using dendritic length from SR and SO layers (Tecuatl et al., 2021).

CA3 interneurons are excluded from the projection model (they do not target CA1), while CA1 interneurons will be added in future updates.
